Boiler repair and fault finding

If your boiler has stopped working, we find the fault and repair it. Repairs and fault finding are £95 for the first hour, then £100 per hour after that. Parts are extra where needed.

Common boiler problems

  • No heating or no hot water
  • Boiler losing pressure
  • Strange noises such as banging or whistling
  • Error codes on the display
  • Radiators not heating up properly

Boiler servicing

A regular service helps keep your boiler safe and efficient and can spot problems before they become breakdowns. A standard boiler service and gas check is £95. A full strip down service, where the boiler is taken apart and cleaned thoroughly, is £175.

Smell gas? Do not use any switches. Open windows, turn off the supply and call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999.
